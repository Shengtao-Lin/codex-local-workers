## Gate and interpretation

Keep every initial failure in the original denominator. Narrow, finding-driven
inherited rework is allowed by the normal supervised policy, but its recovery
rate is reported separately and cannot make an original autonomous pass.
No repeated unchanged packet, no reference-code injection, no test weakening,
no swapping out failed tasks for easier tasks, and no unlimited prompt escalation.

Formal qualification requires all six tasks accepted twice after deterministic
validation, independent Reviewer and risk-routed Primary review; Explorer must
independently succeed in all four required investigations. The two dependency
chains require verified Primary producer acceptance before consumer dispatch and
independent final integration. Reviewer must correctly adjudicate all eight
controls. An unfinished, failed or unreviewed cell cannot satisfy this gate.
The initial-pass matrix and guided recovery matrix remain distinct; recovery
does not by itself establish repeated independent capability.

Only qualified capability families may be offered to an isolated Coordinator
test. No default activation, model change or production promotion follows from
one successful cohort. Primary/cloud effort remains unknown unless actually
measured; model wall time does not prove cloud-cost savings.
