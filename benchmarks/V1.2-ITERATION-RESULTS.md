# v1.2 post-fix stability iteration — 2026-09-26

This is a post-fix comparison to the immutable first baseline in
`V1.2-UNIT-BASELINE.md`, not a rewrite of that baseline. The final frozen
batch is `work/stability-v1/batch-e0d7e51fbedb/summary.json`: six pinned
fixtures, two independent snapshots per fixture, 12 first-packet units. The
source fixtures remain copied from the two read-only sibling repositories.
No runtime, runner, model, or configuration change occurred during this batch.

| Role | Model | Context | Evidence success |

| Coder | `qwen/qwen3-coder-30b` (Q4_K_M) | 24,576 | 12/12 `ready_for_review` |

All three used `http://127.0.0.1:12345/v1`. The six cases were
`selection-order`, `mapping-metadata`, `metadata-limit`,
`reviewer-citation`, `internal-trace`, and the two-file `sample-identity`.
Each passed both rounds, including independent pytest, Ruff format and lint.
Protocol E2E was **12/12**, compared with **5/12** in the first baseline and
**10/12** in the intermediate post-fix batch
`work/stability-v1/batch-326ac70bbfc1/`. Infrastructure failures were
**0/12**. Primary inspected every actual cumulative diff and recorded 12
immutable `accept` decisions in the final run archives; the high-risk
`reviewer-citation` source and protected test received full review.

The final batch issued 10 repair focuses. The archived runner reported the
next action as an edit in 5/10, but that metric omitted one successful
`SAFE_REPLACE_LINE`; direct event audit gives **6/10**. All ten affected
units ultimately reached successful validation and review. Immediate
focus-to-edit conversion improved only modestly from the first baseline's
9/17, while eventual repair success improved substantially. The original
`summary.json` is retained unchanged; future batches use the corrected
metric.
Both high-risk `reviewer-citation` reports converged on valid source
references and acknowledged successful approved focused-test and Ruff runs.
The Reviewer still incurred four protocol errors across the 12 units (two
in round-1 `selection-order`, one in each `reviewer-citation` round), all
recovered within the bounded retry budget. The final batch does not prove
first-report reliability. The terminal gate rejected one post-validation
action in each `sample-identity` round and then obtained a legal finish.

Reported input-token accounting from canonical model-request events was
136,602 Explorer (51 requests), 463,515 Coder (111), and 367,940 Reviewer
(77). Output-token totals were unavailable. Maximum reported context

20 requests each; this is an efficiency concern despite passing. Summed

This iteration included an Explorer observed-line citation gate, bounded
Coder repair/post-edit action supervision, guarded single-line editing, a
one-shot `contract_check` scenario-id shape repair, Reviewer report-only
nudging, and stronger protected metadata-limit tests. The final
`sample-identity` packet gives narrower, advisory guidance for its two
existing one-line calls. Intermediate probes and failed runs remain in
their own archives; their rates are not pooled with the final batch.

**Decision:** the current role mapping and runtime are ready for a
reproducible v1.2 local-unit baseline and further comparison, without
switching models. This is **not** evidence for enabling live v2.1
Coordinator routing yet: the sample is six fixture types repeated twice,
Reviewer first-report errors remain, and immediate repair-focus-to-edit
conversion improved only modestly. The next evaluation should add
more distinct frozen real units and keep Explorer, Coder, Reviewer, Primary
acceptance, and infrastructure denominators separate. Public benchmark
cases remain the second layer after local replayability.
