## Explorer OSS20B versus Bonsai-27B

The frozen read-only paired trial
`work/stability-v1/explorer-ab-bonsai/` compared both models on
`selection-order`, `internal-trace`, and two-file `sample-identity`. An

`work/stability-v1/explorer-ab-internal-repeat/`. All ten model/case

Manual semantic audit differs sharply from that mechanical score. Both

defects in the first paired round. On `internal-trace`, Bonsai initially
omitted the faulty service-membership branch, then described `not in` as

reliable semantic diagnosis for this fixture. The observed wall times also

unsuitable as a definitive latency benchmark.

**Decision:** do not replace OSS with Bonsai on these data. More importantly,
report `Explorer valid evidence` separately from `Explorer correct diagnosis`.
The expanded E2E baseline's 11/11 Explorer metric is the former only; it
must not be interpreted as a measured 11/11 semantic success rate. Future
semantic qualification should use a Primary-reviewed finding checklist or
an independently verified oracle, not line-citation presence alone.

locations, and input labels but no answers. Explorer can record one prediction,
expectation, verdict, and actually observed source/test quote per ID via
